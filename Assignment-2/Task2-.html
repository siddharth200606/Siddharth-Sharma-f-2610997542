5. College Terminology

A teaching session conducted by a professor or teacher.
Assignment
A task given to students to complete as part of their studies.
Semester
A division of the academic year into a specific period of study.
Sessional
An internal examination or assessment conducted during the semester.
Attendance
The record of a student's presence in college classes.
