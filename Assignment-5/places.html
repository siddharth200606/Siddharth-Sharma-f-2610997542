Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is a famous monument located in Agra. It is known for its beautiful architecture and is one of the most famous tourist attractions in India.

India Gate

India Gate is a famous war memorial located in New Delhi. It is one of the most popular landmarks in the capital city.

Gateway of India

The Gateway of India is a historic monument located in Mumbai. It is situated near the Arabian Sea and is a popular tourist spot.

Hawa Mahal

Hawa Mahal is a beautiful palace located in Jaipur. It is famous for its unique architecture and many windows.

Manali

Manali is a beautiful hill station in Himachal Pradesh. It is famous for mountains, valleys and scenic views.
