About Indian Cuisine

Indian cuisine is famous around the world for its variety of flavors, spices and traditional cooking methods. Every region of India has its own special dishes and unique style of cooking. Indian food includes spicy curries, delicious street food, traditional meals and tasty desserts.

Popular Dishes

Biryani
A flavorful rice dish prepared with spices, vegetables or meat.
Masala Dosa
A crispy South Indian dish made from fermented rice and lentil batter and served with potato filling.
Gulab Jamun
A popular Indian dessert made from milk solids and soaked in sweet sugar syrup.
